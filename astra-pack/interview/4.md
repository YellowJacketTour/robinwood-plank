Guidance for Astra — Pine and PlankCaster
What “one sign-in” means
Inside Plank Love: one wallet session covers porch, satchel, WoodAmp, pine composer, Charmville. No repeated wallet modal for ordinary acts. That session is SIWE / existing challenge-session, not Twitter OAuth.
Across foreign apps: the human signs into X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat, etc. once per machine, inside PlankCaster, on those sites’ real login pages. Plank Love never sees those passwords. After that, choosing destinations on a pine does not mean logging into Meta again.
Those are two different sign-ins. Do not collapse them into one OAuth dialog.
Can OAuth be the access point and the post-out channel?
No, not for all platforms, not forever, not free, not unlimited.

Rules:

Passwords and site cookies never touch InMotion Postgres.
Intent is wallet-signed: pine id, body hash, destination list, stamp id, timestamp. Caster refuses unsigned intents.
Stamp burns when the intent is accepted, including failed send.
Status back to Plank Love is honest: queued / sent / blocked-by-site / caster-offline. No fake checkmarks.
No server-side headless farm of sessions. If the caster is closed, pines wait on the device, not on our host.
Desktop is the real caster. Phone slice 1: compose and queue on plank.love; flush when the caster is next open. Optional later: OS share sheet as a manual dock, still stamp-on-intent. Do not pretend a mobile WebView on our domain is a logged-in Instagram.

Product rule
textprofile (handle / board / Charmville owner)
  ├── primary wallet     session, porch receipts, pine intents
  ├── linked wallets     inventory, listings, display, optional “use this key”
  └── each link          namespace + address + provider + proof + visibility

Primary is who the session is. Harvest, stamp, satchel writes, pine intent signatures.
Linked are first-class citizens for “this is also me”: RobinWood on another key, SOL bag, BTC ordinal, a Vault wallet, a phone wallet.
Switching primary is an explicit signed act, not a silent AppKit accountChanged.
Unlink is signed by that key or by primary.
Hidden links exist (parked doc). Public links can show as pills on the board.
A linked key cannot attach to a second profile. If it tries, refuse.

Charmville stays on the profile, not on whichever injected account MetaMask last blinked. Wallets are hosted by the board.

User connects wallet A (injected or WC). SIWE / SIWX into the profile session.

Guidance paragraph to paste
A PlankSpace profile hosts many wallets from many providers. WalletConnect / Reown AppKit (current AppKit, ethers adapter, EIP-6963 + WC QR, Solana/Bitcoin adapters where downtown already speaks those chains) is how keys walk in. Postgres plankspace_wallet_links is how they stay. Primary key owns the session, Charmville, satchel writes, and pine intents. Linked keys prove inventory and sign for assets they hold. A key belongs to at most one profile. Reown Multiwallet Linking is an optional hot-session upgrade, not the source of truth. Official X/IG OAuth is not a wallet provider.
