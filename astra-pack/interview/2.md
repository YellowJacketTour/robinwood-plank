## 1. Name the collision away

Keep the overlap only where language already lives.

- **Grain** = a post. “What’s on your grain?” does not move.
- **grain** = the soft currency. Lowercase in UI chrome is fine. Never a stack in the satchel.
- **Sheaf 🌾** = the harvestable face. Inventory id `sheaf`. Not `grain`. Not `grain_token`.

Sheaf is the right word: it is the bundle you walk off the plot with. Kernel or Stalk also work; Sheaf reads as *harvested*. Splinter / Knock / Hum / Pith / Gleam / Knot stay.

Three ledgers, always: `grain_balance`, `satchel(sheaf)`, `posts`. If a report ever adds them together, the report is wrong.

## 2. Signing: session for the porch, pen for the street

FarmVille never asked for a credit card to pick a strawberry. SimCity never asked you to notarize a zone. The constitution needs a **bound owner**, not a modal per click.

- Board owner already has the PlankSpace wallet session (`/api/auth/challenge`). That session covers plant, harvest, compost, stamp, socket, neighbor-tend, satchel moves. Each action writes a receipt keyed to that wallet. No popup.
- **Explicit signature** when value can leave the porch: GE list/fill, wrap, caster-dock send, anything quoted in `$PLANK`.
- Brand-new visitor with no wallet: they can *look* at porches. They cannot own plots. Do not invent an email farm account. PlankSpace is already wallet-owned. Bootstrap is “connect to claim your lot,” same as “connect to post.”
- If `docs/marketplank/DECISION-wallet-session-keys.md` gives you scoped session keys, use that family for porch actions. Do not invent a second auth.

Walkthrough and constitution now agree: the first five minutes never show a sign modal. Listing does.

## 3. Ready window and the week-away porch

**Ripe window: grow time + 48 hours.**
Sheaf: 4h grow, then 48h harvestable. Splinter: 16h grow, then 48h. After that, compost: plot kept, **zero faces**, seed returned to the satchel at 1:1. That is fallow-with-your-seed-back, not Unwither and not a wipe.

**Week away:** do not print a magic grain faucet.

- Compost already hands the seed back, so empty-grain cannot trap you if you had planted.
- Starter kit: first-time lot gets 2 Sheaf seeds + 1 Splinter seed. Not refillable from nowhere.
- A **porch tin** of at most 2 Sheaf seeds, refilled only from compost leftovers or from a neighbor gift, never from a daily timer. Bound = 2.
- After seven quiet days the lot looks sleepy (plots empty, shed still there, one package if a neighbor left a tend-gift). No weeds-as-shame. No “your city abandoned” advisor. Replant from tin + returned seeds.

Humane means you can start again. It does not mean the city pays you for vanishing.

## 4. Social is makeup and a small bonus, never the engine

Every solitary board must feed its owner.

Land value:

\[
V = V_{\min} + (1-V_{\min})\cdot\mathrm{clamp}(\text{care, visits, civic, use} - \text{glut})
\]

Set \(V_{\min}\) high enough that a quiet newcomer still gets a real Sheaf. Example target: solo harvest ≈ 80–90% of a loved-board harvest, not 20%. Visits and WoodAmp must not be a paywall.

Knock and Hum:

- **Always** change appearance (Knock plots look more “visited”; Hum plots pulse if WoodAmp is on).
- **May** add a capped yield crumb, e.g. +0–15% faces, hard cap, distinct spenders/visits only, ignored if sybil-shaped.
- Never freeze growth because nobody came.

Baseline care (logging in, planting, harvesting) is enough to stay at \(V_{\min}\). That is SimCity’s small town that still gets power, not a city that dies without tourists.

## 5. Phone is one window; desktop is a desk

Yes. That split is the feeling.

- Phone: porch stays inline under the composer. Expanded lot is full-screen touch placement. Satchel **or** market in the foreground, chip to swap. No overlapping XP windows at 390px. DESIGN.md already lives at that width.
- Desktop: pouch and market are draggable, can coexist with WoodAmp, Unicorn-desk energy.

The porch thumbnail on the board is the same object on both. You are not shipping two games.

FarmVille mobile worked because it was one farm surface. Unicorn’s magic was windows on a desk. Honor both by **viewport**, not by cutting the bag out of phones.
