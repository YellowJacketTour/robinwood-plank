01 · The flight curves upward

The multiplier starts at 1× and grows about 24% every second. The crash can come at any moment. Half of all flights end below 2×.

You choose when to lock. Lock before the crash and you are a survivor at that multiplier. Lock too late and you bust: your stake is gone for that round. The crash point is fixed before launch from a sealed random value, so nobody, including the house, can move it once the flight starts.

formula multiplier after t seconds: x(t) = e^(0.22·t)
chance the flight reaches at least m: about 1 ÷ m
x(t)=e0.22t
Marked points: 2× at t=3s, 9× at t=10s. The curve never slows down — the crash ends it, not the shape of the flight.

02 · What am I putting in?

Your stake — a 4.50% rake, no per-lock fees, no hidden edge in the draw.

Rake falls 0.25% for every 25,000,000 credits the table has ever wagered, down to a 2.50% floor. The player pot rises from 95.5% of stakes today toward 97.5%. Current rate: shown in the game header.

formula rake = max(2.50%, 4.50% − 0.25% × ⌊lifetime volume ÷ 25,000,000⌋)
player pot = stakes × (1 − rake) = 95.5% of stakes today

Where every credit of rake goes

25%$PLANK buy & burn Rake buys real $PLANK on the open market and burns it. The burn engine can only burn what it buys; nobody receives it.
69%Community: Powerboard + Vault Split between the lottery prize and the protected vault — see below.
6%Operations Running the table. Fixed share, disclosed here.
as a share of every stake rake 4.50% = $PLANK buy-and-burn 1.125% + community 3.105% + operations 0.27%

Minimum stake is 500 credits. Test credits in the playtest have no cash value.

03 · What can I get back on a flight?

Two parts: a guaranteed floor, plus a weighted share of whatever is left. Your lock multiplier is claim weight, not a fixed payout promise. Bust and you get 0.

  • Survivor floor — 75% of stake, guaranteed. Locked before the crash, this is yours no matter what anyone else does.
  • Performance premium. What's left of the pot after every floor, split by weight = stake × ln(your multiplier). Higher locks and bigger stakes weigh more.
  • House bonus (optional, can be 0). Only from table reserve, capped at fair odds. Never taken from other players.
formula pot = stakes × 95.5%
weight_i = stake_i × ln(m_i)
premium = pot − 0.75 × Σ survivor stakes
payout_i = 0.75 × stake_i + premium × weight_i ÷ Σ weights
Weight per credit staked, by lock multiplier. Doubling your multiplier does not double your weight — the log curve rewards surviving longer, but with diminishing returns.

Worked example: 10 players, 1,000 credits each, you lock at 2.0×

Stakes 10,000 → rake 450 → pot 9,550. Four survive: two at 1.5×, you at 2.0×, one at 3.0×. Six bust.

04 · How does the lottery work and what does it cost me?

Nothing extra. Every stake is automatically a ticket, funded from rake you already paid.

  • Funding. The community's 69% rake share splits, fixed on-chain, 65/35: 65% to the Powerboard prize (pot × 2.02%), 35% to the protected vault (pot × 1.09%). Same split on every table — playtest and mainnet alike.
  • Your ticket weight is your total stake in the current prize epoch. Bigger stake, bigger odds.
  • The draw. On a funded round, one of 16 balls is drawn from a pre-committed reveal: the number comes from a sealed random value published before the round, so the house cannot choose it. Ball 1 is the hit.
  • The winner. On a hit, the sealed prize is paid to one player chosen proportionally to ticket weight, from the same committed randomness.
formula your odds this draw = (your weight ÷ everyone's weight) × 1/16
prize funding per round = pot × 0.045 × 0.69 × 0.65 ≈ pot × 0.0202
vault funding per round = pot × 0.045 × 0.69 × 0.35 ≈ pot × 0.0109

When can a draw actually happen?

Only once two amounts are fully sealed: the current prize, and the reset reserve for the next one. A win can never empty the board. Each hit ratchets the next base prize up by 5% or 50,000 credits, whichever is bigger — never down. A 10% ops fee applies to the funding, so credits needed = target ÷ 0.90.

formula credits needed to seal a prize = target ÷ 0.90
draw allowed when: prize sealed AND reset reserve ≥ (next base ÷ 0.90)
next base = max(base × 1.05, base + 50,000)
rounds to seal ≈ credits needed ÷ (pot × 0.018 × routed share)

05 · What does the whole table's money do?

Out of every 100 credits staked across all players: 95.5 go back to survivors, 1.125 buy and burn $PLANK, 2.02 fund the Powerboard prize, 1.09 grow the protected vault, and 0.27 pay for operations.

95.5%Player purse Returned to that round's survivors, every round, exactly.
1.125%$PLANK buy & burn Permanently removed. Total supply only goes down from play.
2.02%Powerboard prize Accumulates until a prize and its reset reserve are sealed.
1.09%Protected vault Never decreases. See below for how it makes future rounds more generous.
0.27%Operations The 6% share of rake.

The protected vault and the prize base only go up

  • Protected vault. A reserve that can only grow, funded only from routed rake: 35% of the community's 69% share, i.e. pot × 1.09% every round. A brand-new table starts at 0 and the vault grows from its first settled round; it never decreases.
  • Prize base. The guaranteed reset base for the lottery never decreases; each hit ratchets it upward.

The table gets more generous the more it has been played

Two curves key off rounds ever played — never stake size, never the vault's own balance. One large deposit cannot buy its way up either curve; only repeated play can. Both climb only, never back.

House bonus cap: 25% × (1 − 0.999n). Narrows house risk budget only — never widens what a player is owed.
Lottery reseed ceiling: base → 10× base, same 0.999-per-round curve. A mature table's winners keep more of a given prize than a brand-new table's would.

Past 4,000 contributing rounds a curve is already ~98% saturated — squeezing out its last fraction of a percent does less than accelerating a still-climbing sibling. Once a game passes that point, its further rounds credit the other game's curve instead. Nothing here ever touches either vault's balance; only the two growth curves cross over.

formula bonus cap(n) = 25% × (1 − 0.999n), n = rounds ever contributed
reseed ceiling(n) = ceiling − (ceiling − base) × 0.999n
past 4,000 rounds: further rounds credit the OTHER game's n instead invariants checked every round vault(t+1) ≥ vault(t) · prize base(t+1) ≥ prize base(t) · both curves monotonic in n · Σ payouts ≤ funded capacity

06 · What is guaranteed vs what varies

Guaranteed

  • Solvency every round: payouts never exceed what is funded.
  • 75% of your stake back if you lock before the crash.
  • The lottery pays only when the prize and its reset reserve are fully sealed.
  • Crash point, drawn ball and winner all come from committed randomness published before the round.
  • Fixed 30-second cadence between launches.
  • Splitting a stake across wallets earns no extra claim.
  • The vault and the lottery's reseed curve only ever grow; neither can regress.
  • Both growth curves key off rounds played, never off stake size, so no single deposit can buy a bigger share of either.

Varies

  • Your outcome: it depends on when you lock and what everyone else does.
  • How fast the prize grows: it scales with the table's pot size.
  • The crash point, every round.
  • The rake rate over time (only downward, with volume).
  • The house bonus, which may be zero, and its ceiling, which climbs as the table matures.

2026-09-05: an internal review found two loopholes that let a player running two coordinated wallets manufacture cheap rounds to farm the lottery odds, or a two-wallet "solo table" siphon from the house's own per-round seed. Neither could touch a single honest player's payout or anyone else's stake — both required deliberately running multiple accounts against the house. Both were closed the same day: the lottery's hit odds are now tied to what a round actually pays into rake, and the house bonus is capped to a share of that same round's rake.
