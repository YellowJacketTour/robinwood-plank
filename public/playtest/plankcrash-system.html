01 · THE FLIGHT

Timing creates claim weight—not a fixed-odds promise.

The displayed multiplier follows an exponential clock, x(t)=e0.22t. A player survives only when the accepted lock precedes the committed crash. Locking later increases settlement weight, but also increases the probability of losing the stake.

Survival law: Pr(crash ≥ m) = 1/m  ·  cumulative hazard: H(m) = −ln(1/m) = ln(m)

This is why the settlement weight uses wᵢ=sᵢ·ln(mᵢ): stake measures capital exposed; ln(m) measures endured crash hazard. The two quantities are continuous, so there are no artificial multiplier buckets to game.

02 · PLAYER POOL

Survivors divide what players supplied.

After the disclosed tiered rake (4.50% initially, permanently declining toward 2.50%), the player layer is fully distributed to survivors. Each survivor first receives a 75% stake floor when feasible; the remaining premium clears pro rata by hazard weight.

pᵢ = f·sᵢ + λ·sᵢ·ln(mᵢ), with λ chosen so Σpᵢ = player distributable

No survivor is promised stake × multiplier. Performance changes relative claim on a finite pool.

03 · HOUSE LAYER

A bounded bonus, never an open liability.

A separate seed may add upside. Its total availability is globally capped at 10% of reserve-at-lock, then divided by the same linear stake × hazard weight and constrained by additive fair-odds caps.

Havail=min(H, 10%·reserveAtLock)  ·  bonusᵢ≤sᵢ(mᵢ−1)

Because every constraint is identity-independent and homogeneous in stake, splitting one position among wallets cannot increase its aggregate economic claim.

04 · CONSERVATION

The ledger closes every round.

Player stakes, any bounded seed, payouts, rake and reserve returns reconcile exactly in integer credits. If everyone crashes, the distributable is returned to the reserve. Unused house capacity also returns to reserve rather than becoming an undisclosed fee.

player pool + seed = survivor payouts + routed rake + reserve return
05 · THE VAULT

Durability is a first-class game state.

The Vault is protected principal and bounded emission capacity, not a promise that every round can pay every displayed multiplier. Caps limit exposure; unspent capacity returns; designated overflow can fund the independent Powerboard path without entering crash-round risk calculations.

06 · POWERBOARD

Every qualified flight advances a slower, rarer payoff.

A defined share of the community allocation funds the Powerboard. A prize seals only when its stated target is fully funded. Each eligible player receives linear voucher weight from qualified participation; within a winning epoch, prize ownership is proportional to voucher weight, so dividing the same stake among identities creates no extra weight.

voucher shareᵢ = weightᵢ / Σweight  ·  combined chanceᵢ = draw-hit probability × voucher shareᵢ

The draw displays one sphere for every equally eligible result. In the current 16-ball configuration, each committed result has probability 1/16. A miss rolls the funded prize forward. A hit pays the selected voucher winner, preserves a reset reserve, and begins the next prize cycle from its defined base.

DESIGN BASIS

Why this structure

Parimutuel markets make realized returns depend on the whole field, not a bookmaker’s fixed promise. That property gives PlankCrash a naturally bounded player layer. The continuous hazard transform rewards endured risk without creating discrete timing cliffs. Global, additive constraints keep economics invariant to wallet partitioning. The second prize loop turns repeated rounds into a compounding communal objective while keeping lottery liabilities separately funded.

Reference foundations: Terrell & Farmer, optimal betting and efficiency in parimutuel markets; Whelan, market structure and returns in betting markets; Zechar & Zhuang, parimutuel redistribution and bounded returns; IRTF RFC 9381, verifiable-random-function properties and limitations. The formulas above describe the active public playtest policy and its deterministic settlement implementation.
