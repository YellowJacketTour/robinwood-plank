01 · The flight curves upward

The multiplier starts at 1× and grows about 24% every second. The crash can come at any moment. Half of all flights end below 2×.

You choose when to lock. Lock before the crash and you are a survivor at that multiplier. Lock too late and you bust: your stake is gone for that round. The crash point is fixed before launch from a sealed random value, so nobody, including the house, can move it once the flight starts.

formula multiplier after t seconds: x(t) = e^(0.22·t)
chance the flight reaches at least m: about 1 ÷ m

1× at launch · about 2× after 3 seconds · about 9× after 10 seconds. The curve never slows down; the crash ends it.

02 · What am I putting in?

Your stake, and nothing else. The only cost is a 4.50% rake taken from the table's stakes each round. There are no per-lock fees and no hidden edge in the random draw.

The rake rate falls as the table's lifetime volume grows: 0.25% off for every 25,000,000 credits wagered, down to a floor of 2.50%. That means the player pot grows from 95.5% of stakes today toward 97.5%. The table's current rate is shown in the game header.

formula rake = max(2.50%, 4.50% − 0.25% × ⌊lifetime volume ÷ 25,000,000⌋)
player pot = stakes × (1 − rake) = 95.5% of stakes today

Where every credit of rake goes

as a share of every stake rake 4.50% = $PLANK buy-and-burn 1.80% + Powerboard 1.80% + operations 0.90%

03 · What can I get back on a flight?

Survivors share the player pot. There is no fixed payout: the multiplier you lock at is your claim weight, not a promise of stake × multiplier.

Each survivor's payout has two parts:

  • Survivor floor. If you lock before the crash, you get 75% of your stake back, guaranteed.
  • Performance premium. Whatever remains in the player pot after all floors is split among survivors by weight. Your weight is your stake × ln(your lock multiplier). Higher locks and bigger stakes weigh more.
  • House bonus (optional). When the table's reserve holds funds, a bonus can be added on top, capped at fair odds (stake × (multiplier − 1)) and by a global reserve cap. It can be zero; it is never taken from other players.

Busting returns 0.

formula pot = stakes × 95.5%
weight_i = stake_i × ln(m_i)
premium = pot − Σ floors = pot − 0.75 × Σ survivor stakes
payout_i = 0.75 × stake_i + premium × weight_i ÷ Σ weights

04 · How does the lottery work and what does it cost me?

It costs you nothing extra. The Powerboard prize is funded only by the 40% community share of rake. Every stake you place is automatically a ticket.

  • Funding. Every round, the 40% community share of the 4.50% rake (pot × 1.8%) is routed by the table's setting: mainnet routes all of it to the prize; playtest tables route 65% to the prize (pot × 1.17%) and keep 35% compounding the protected vault.
  • Your ticket weight is your total stake in the current prize epoch. Bigger stake, bigger share of the odds.
  • The draw. On a funded round, one of 16 balls is drawn from a pre-committed reveal: the number comes from a sealed random value published before the round, so the house cannot choose it. Ball 1 is the hit.
  • The winner. On a hit, the sealed prize is paid to one player chosen proportionally to ticket weight, from the same committed randomness.
formula your odds this draw = (your weight ÷ everyone's weight) × 1/16
prize funding per round = pot × 0.045 × 0.40 × routed share
  mainnet (100% routed) = pot × 0.018 · playtest (65% routed) = pot × 0.0117

When can a draw actually happen?

A win must never empty the board, so a draw is only allowed once two amounts are fully sealed: the current prize, and the reset reserve for the next prize. After a hit, the next base prize ratchets up by 5% or 50,000 credits, whichever is larger, and can never go down. Sealing takes a 10% operations fee on the funding that constitutes each prize, so the credits needed are the target ÷ 0.90.

formula credits needed to seal a prize = target ÷ 0.90
draw allowed when: prize sealed AND reset reserve ≥ (next base ÷ 0.90)
next base = max(base × 1.05, base + 50,000)
rounds to seal ≈ credits needed ÷ (pot × 0.018 × routed share)

05 · What does the whole table's money do?

Out of every 100 credits staked across all players: 95.5 go back to survivors, 1.8 buy and burn $PLANK, 1.8 fund the Powerboard prize, and 0.9 pay for operations.

95.5%Player purse Returned to that round's survivors, every round, exactly.
1.8%$PLANK buy & burn Permanently removed. Total supply only goes down from play.
1.8%Powerboard prize Accumulates until a prize and its reset reserve are sealed.
0.9%Operations The 20% share of rake.

The protected vault and the prize base only go up

  • Protected vault. A reserve that can only grow, funded only from routed rake: half of whatever community share is not sent to the prize. On playtest tables 35% of the community share is kept back, so the vault receives pot × 0.315% every round (the other half of that kept share seeds future flights). A brand-new table starts at 0 and the vault grows from its first settled round; it never decreases.
  • Prize base. The guaranteed reset base for the lottery never decreases; each hit ratchets it upward.
invariants checked every round vault(t+1) ≥ vault(t) · prize base(t+1) ≥ prize base(t) · Σ payouts ≤ funded capacity

06 · What is guaranteed vs what varies

Guaranteed

  • Solvency every round: payouts never exceed what is funded.
  • 75% of your stake back if you lock before the crash.
  • The lottery pays only when the prize and its reset reserve are fully sealed.
  • Crash point, drawn ball and winner all come from committed randomness published before the round.
  • Fixed 30-second cadence between launches.
  • Splitting a stake across wallets earns no extra claim.

Varies

  • Your outcome: it depends on when you lock and what everyone else does.
  • How fast the prize grows: it scales with the table's pot size.
  • The crash point, every round.
  • The rake rate over time (only downward, with volume).
  • The house bonus, which may be zero.
