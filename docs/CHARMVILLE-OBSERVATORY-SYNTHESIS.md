# Observatory and almanac synthesis

2026-09-08. The supplied speculative manual is inspiration, not a replacement
for verified research, accepted product decisions or implemented behavior.

## Adopt into the design

The useful progression is cell → porch → board → neighborhood → destinations.
Each scale adds a reason to use the same home. A small completed garden should
feel satisfying before expansion exists. Music, ornaments and visitors give the
board personality; crop production supplies expressive goods for that social
space. Keep this relationship visible through composition and action feedback.

Use five questions when studying a reference: how space fits together, what an
inventory holds, how time works, what visitors may do, and which goods or keys
authorize actions. Record observed behavior separately from proposed adaptations.
Source models, sprites and software remain useful production inputs under their
recorded terms; this method does not reduce research to abstract prose alone.

Author the first session as a sequence: recognize home and ownership, notice ripe
beds, harvest, see the distinct charm and returned seed, replant, open the bag,
stamp a pine and see its reaction count change. Show the server-confirmed result
before celebrating it. Session expiry gets a clear recovery path. The board and
music remain mounted through garden and satchel interactions.

## Expansion without silently changing the first slice

| Layer | Concrete next value | Dependencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Garden | Coherent isometric scene, distinct crops and charms, responsive action feedback | Art pipeline and live visual review |
| Expression | Satchel stacks become stamps and later placed ornaments | Accepted server events and explicit placement rules |
| Discovery | Current/lifetime totals and unique supporters; combined thresholds and sorts | Reaction projections, permissions and content adapters |
| Neighborhood | Recognizable visits, saved decoration and richer presence | Owner/visitor permissions; layout and presence contracts |
| Market | Bag and book as sibling windows, discoverable catalog and offers | Dedicated market specification; no public book in slice 1 |
| Destinations | Compose here, dispatch through the human's caster | Primary identity, accepted intents and honest recovery |
| Seasons/crafting | New combinations, collections and shared events | Explicit recipes, provenance, costs and anti-duplication rules |

The four reputation metrics remain separate: current face totals, lifetime face
totals, current unique supporters and lifetime unique supporters. Inventory is
not a fifth reputation metric. A catalog item's art, tradability, ornament use
and reputation semantics need explicit fields rather than inference from rarity.
