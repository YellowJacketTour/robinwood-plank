02

Three layers

Collection · Index · Energy. Lean stack — no dual tokens.

L1 · COLLECTION

Vault share (S)

One ERC-20 per collection. Always compounding. Liquid on AMM.

  • In: NFT → mint share
  • Out: burn share → NFT
  • Trade: share ↔ WETH
  • Fees: raise claim behind every share
L2 · INDEX

Vault of vaults

Basket of collection shares + WETH. Your iToken is a slice of all of it.

  • Mint: deposit full pro-rata basket
  • Redeem: burn iToken → every asset
  • Weights: real fees & demand — not floors
  • Growth: Bus buys more shares in
L3 · ENERGY

Automatic pipes

WETH fees that hit the Bus split into fixed routes. No free iToken mint.

  • Art: buy shares into index
  • Depth: locked LP that keeps earning
  • Scarce: burn/lock IDX & PLANK
  • DRIP: yield reinvests by default

05

Index: going in & coming out

No oracle NAV. Full basket in · full slice out.

→ Mint iToken

  1. Pro-rata path: deposit a fair slice of every collection share + WETH the index holds.
  2. Convenience path: pay WETH; a solver buys the basket and mints in one atomic flow (you set min out).
  3. Result: you receive iTokens. Fees never print free iTokens to fee payers.
Costs: gas · AMM spreads if using a solver · no floor-oracle mint tax on core.

← Redeem iToken

  1. Burn iTokens on the core contract anytime.
  2. Receive a pro-rata slice of every share + WETH in the vault.
  3. Optional: sell shares on collection AMMs or redeem NFTs from vaults.
Costs: gas · later AMM/NFT fees if you unwind · exit never waits on an oracle or admin.
06

How PLANK is affected

Automatic — only from real Bus WETH. Never a redeemable NFT-index asset.

Buy & burn

10%

Of every WETH unit on the Energy Bus, 10% market-buys PLANK and burns it. Triggered whenever vault activity feeds the Bus and route() runs. Scarcity for remaining PLANK holders — funded by NFT market use.

Add LP & lock

10%

Another 10% of Bus WETH builds PLANK/WETH liquidity and locks it (no admin withdraw). Fees on that depth can harvest into more PLANK burns and more Bus energy. Tighter PLANK market over time — still outside the NFT basket.

09

Hard rules

No dual tokens

One compounding share per collection. No “forget to stake.”

No settlement oracle

Mint/redeem use balances and pro-rata math only.

No free iToken mint

Fees fatten claims and depth — they don’t print free index shares.

Exit always open

Pro-rata redeem cannot be bricked by feeds, admins, or keepers.
