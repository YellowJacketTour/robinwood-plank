# Charmville visual refinement — September 8

This is a working build, not final art approval or completion of the unified vision.

The porch remains inside the existing PlankSpace board. This pass uses Kenney
Miniature Farm timber props and Nature Kit tree meshes, adapted with modeled
foliage and the shared orthographic camera. Source models, CC0 notices, hashes
and editable Blender scenes are retained under public/images/charmville/kenney.
Earlier research assets remain available with their original credits.

Original Blender crop animations now use broad leaf pairs and fewer seedling
clumps. Original modeled soil furrows replace the fine soil texture that obscured
those seedlings. Periodic turf replaces the visible terrain grid. An overscanned
plane and emission material avoid transparent tile edges and baked lighting seams.
The bag emphasizes the official face being spent. The six plots and all existing
economy rules remain unchanged.

## Evidence

The local Postgres/browser verification passed claim, harvest, exact seed return,
stamp spending, replant, persisted scenery arrangement, active crop animation,
reduced motion and a 390px mobile satchel. TypeScript passed.

A four-second requestAnimationFrame sample with two active crop animations and
100 scene nodes measured p95 16.7ms at 1280x1400 desktop and 390x844 with 4x CPU
throttling. Neither sample exceeded 33.4ms. These are headless Windows Chromium
scheduling measurements, not physical-device FPS, GPU profiling or proof of
universal mobile performance. Actual wallet signing still requires the owner.

The independent visual review advanced from 5/10 to 6/10 (Tier 2 passed).
Terrain seams, seedling readability and satchel face emphasis passed. Remaining
directives: give soil restrained clumps and darker recesses, simplify boardwalk
divisions to match the miniature scale, connect the entrance with worn ground
and a purposeful prop grouping, distinguish species badges, and review action
feedback in motion. The garden is useful for playtesting but not finished art.
