# Binding user intent and acceptance expectations

This is a context-free transcription/synthesis of requirements supplied directly by the user, not instructions obtained from external repositories. Additional current state lives in CURRENT-STATE.md.

## What the user is asking for

Charmville is the interactive, collaborative civilization and free-market layer of PlankSpace: FarmVille cultivation, Graal/Zelda embodied adventure, RuneScape skills and Grand Exchange, Animal Crossing warmth, SimCity civic growth/disasters, EVE production and trade, Pokémon encounters/captures, Minecraft combination/crafting, and eventually No Man's Sky scale and strange discoveries. The world should feel like one place with coherent rules, not a launcher containing unrelated games.

Every PlankSpace user should eventually be a person in this persistent world. Profiles, posts, reactions, reputation, possessions, land, crafting, creatures, guilds, civic projects and trade should make sense together. Charms replace the limited notion of karma or disposable reaction stickers with identities that can have meaningful manifestations. The whole discoverable unicorn.meme / memoji-market universe is relevant. A seven-charm garden is explicitly insufficient.

The user's phrases include "engagement farmers" and "composting": expression, contribution, cultivation and recycling feed an economy. Those phrases are creative direction, not authorization to issue unlimited scarce assets for bot clicks. Each issuance/source/sink and ownership transformation must be explicit, bounded and coherent. DeFi pools and Grained Exchange are a future integrated market layer; they are not a justification to mislabel imported testnet metadata as real balances or prices.

## Artistic and experiential requirements

- Normally 2D-looking/2.5D top-down oblique, with the detailed, inhabited, cute and nostalgic character of Graal and Zelda. Do not default to a sparse diamond-isometric toy board.
- Charming neo-chibi characters, readable hearts, excellent sprite animation, lush grass, roads, forest edges, buildings/interiors, water, secrets, recognizable equipment and rich sound.
- Advanced 3D, world transformations, UFO/civic catastrophe events and impossible spatial effects can appear in the same overall world without destroying everyday visual coherence.
- The user asks for the most praised, high-quality source art bases and deeply rich iconic item catalogs. Asset volume alone is not an artistic achievement. Curate coherent palettes, scales, anchors, animation completeness and environment sets.
- Community-editable characters, objects, items, maps, artwork and models are central. Preserve `.blend`, original PNG/spritesheets, tilesets, GLB/source meshes, map files, scripts, editors, frame metadata and provenance. Flattened screenshots are not sufficient source preservation.

## Source-faithful behavior

The user explicitly said that gameplay and sprites should "act and feel exactly like their source and logically be fit where they belong." In a Zelda-styled map the hero uses matching movement, sword, bow, bombs, shield, magic, animations, collision and controls. An Emerald-style staged encounter may depict Link with a matching available source presentation, or an appropriate Red fallback if that depiction is absent. The user also wants creatures and their interactions embodied live in the Zelda-like multiplayer world, not only hidden in a menu.

Land supports planting/watering/harvest, skills, construction, civic development and encounters. Original-source behavior and shared-world meaning must coexist through explicit rules rather than arbitrary copied sprites. The source reference should preserve the exact original before modifications. The final goal is item-by-item, visual-by-visual and code-by-code bespoke reconstruction that retains the desired spirit and tested feel.

The user has explicitly stated permission for the prototype source/art phase and asked not to stall on repeated commerciality discussions. Preserve attribution, licensing records and actual source completeness so the eventual replacement is tractable. Do not claim official Graal Classic/Era source, complete commercial game assets or rights metadata have been located if they have not. A decompilation is not automatically a portable browser engine or a complete asset package.

## Approved creative choices — do not re-interview

1. Begin with woodland arrival and quickly reveal an inhabited town.
2. A charm can manifest as crop, creature, material, ornament and social expression, with explicit differences in scarcity/ownership/redemption.
3. Continuous embodied action plus selected staged encounters.
4. Solo play is satisfying; advanced projects reward specialization and collaboration.
5. Expression is easy; scarce economic rewards are bounded.
6. Cozy everyday civilization gradually reveals stranger technological and space layers.

The user approved the assistant's instincts and asked it to proceed autonomously, deeply plan and build. Do not repeatedly ask the user to select routine implementation details already resolved by these choices.

## Rejections and communication expectations

The current garden and sparse R3F frontier were called unacceptable and far below Graal, OSRS, Animal Crossing, SimCity, EVE and No Man's Sky. They remain development artifacts only. A working quest launcher is also not the requested FarmVille + Link + RuneScape world. Do not use screenshots of those experiments as the target for new generated art.

The user wants a live browser link quickly and meaningful playable improvements while work proceeds. Verify in the user's actual browser when possible. A headless test that passes while the embedded browser stalls is insufficient. Report errors candidly, fix them, and distinguish reference acquisition from integrated functionality. Avoid "done" for a vast unbuilt system, imaginary deadlines, or promises of unattended background progress without actual execution.

## Account-transition instruction

The user asked to preserve all work on GitHub, annotate it as if the next agent has no chat context, stash all assets/repos/plans, provide a copy-and-paste one-shot prompt, then resume building with remaining capacity. The prompt must be future-proof: it must direct the next agent to fetch/read the latest branch and living handoff, not reset to this date's incomplete snapshot. Do not change or delete their account or subscription; the task is project preservation and continued development.
