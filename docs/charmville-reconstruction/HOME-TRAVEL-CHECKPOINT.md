## Starter territory design to author

A hollow-tree home opens onto a small porch. Interior needs a bed/return point, personal storage, workbench and decoration placement. Exterior needs the existing six durable garden plots, a water source with a reachable bank, compost container, harvest storage access, a training area and an unobstructed path to the public gate. The entry, garden and gate must be visible or guided from the spawn. Use a coherent sourced treehouse/forest art family, with mapped origins and collision; do not paste mismatched title scenes over the starter map.

The doorstep is a separate interior/exterior transition; the settlement gate crosses private/shared ownership boundaries. Both preserve actor identity and inventory and cancel unfinished actions without undoing committed contact rewards. Home editing cannot move arrival points into solid tiles. Guests need explicit visiting permissions; private ownership cannot be inferred from the destination name supplied by a client.

Public travel expands from a village hub to wilderness, towns, ports and industry. Tower routes require discovery and activation; boats require boarding, navigable water and valid disembarkation; mounts need locomotion/clearance and dismount rules; spacecraft need launch, transit and landable destinations. Every route uses the same location and account model. None of these modes is implemented simply by adding a graph entry.

Next required implementation: authored home map and interior, proven sprite profiles for each tool, native/server account bridge, collision-verified portal bindings, then actual guest visits and public transitions. The requested treehouse territory and wider MMO are unfinished.
