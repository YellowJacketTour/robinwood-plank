# Party commands and shared-world presentation

Charmville should combine direct character control with a legible, configurable companion squad. The useful inheritance is behavioral: recognizable responsibilities, predictable commands, visible preparation, and coherent animation. These references do not establish interchangeable engines or permission to redistribute commercial content. Source evidence and proposed integration are separated below; proposed features are not implementation claims.

## Evidence from the reference games

**Final Fantasy XIV: roles and readable intent.** Square Enix’s job guide separates tanks, healers and damage roles. Tanks attract enemy attention and absorb attacks; healers restore allies and provide mitigation or recovery. This provides a vocabulary for cooperation without requiring every companion to behave identically. The official UI guide allows target HP, statuses and cast progress to be separated and resized. Its interrupt guide identifies a glowing red progress bar as an actionable cue and distinguishes interruptibility from stun resistance. Those are concrete examples of showing what a player can do before an attack resolves. [Job Guide](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/jobguide/battle/), [Noticing Enemy Attacks](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/uiguide/battle/battle-target/ecast_setting.html), [Interrupting Enemy Attacks](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/uiguide/battle/battle-target/ecast_interrupted.html).

FFXIV also teaches party responsibilities through repeatable Hall of the Novice exercises. The official manual includes waymarks, signs, Duty Support and NPC-party systems. These support two distinct lessons: onboarding should rehearse cooperation, and shared spatial information deserves dedicated UI rather than relying on chat. The retrieved pages do not provide an exhaustive ground-marker catalogue, so this report does not claim exact parity with every raid telegraph. [Training for Party Combat](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/uiguide/party/party-how/party_practice.html), [Game Manual](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/game_manual/view/).

**Final Fantasy XII: configurable companions.** Square Enix’s Duncan Heaney describes Gambits as precise conditional behaviors, including healing critically injured allies or prioritizing strong enemies. The April 2020 Zodiac Age update added three interchangeable instruction sets and license resets. The PC manual separately exposes battle commands, party menus, target selection and escape mode. Together these support deliberate tactical overrides alongside comfortable routine behavior. Exact internal priority/cancellation semantics require a dedicated engine-level reference; the proposed rule ordering below is our own specification, not an asserted reproduction. [Square Enix developer article](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2020/04/24/final-fantasy-xii-the-zodiac-age-gets-new-update-on-ps4-today/), [PC manual](https://cdn.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/595520/manuals/FF12_PC_manual_en.pdf?t=1588783777).

**Dragon Ball: assists and transformations with costs.** Bandai Namco’s Sparking! ZERO beginner guide distinguishes HP, Ki and Skill Count. Charging and attacks interact with Ki, while transformations and several defensive maneuvers consume Skill Count. Its September 2024 fighter guide describes switching, transformation selection and fusion; fusion replaces compatible team members with one fused fighter. These are meaningful state changes, not simply an aura graphic. The sources are launch-era design references; exact current balance costs should not be inferred from them. [Beginner’s Guide](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/news/dragon-ball-sparking-zero-the-beginners-guide), [Know Your Fighters](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/news/dragon-ball-sparking-zero-know-your-fighters).

FighterZ’s official version 1.21 notes show character-specific assist variants and detailed adjustments to startup, range, hit effects and recovery. Examples include ranged Kamehameha assists and grounded approach options. The important distinction is between an assist’s tactical purpose and its complete visual timing. A companion appearing near an enemy is not equivalent to a correctly authored assist. [FighterZ 1.21 patch notes](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/news/dragon-ball-fighterz-patch-notes-121).

**Graal: author the whole action.** The platform-hosted Gani tutorial describes composing frames from body, head, shield and imported sprite definitions. Its carrying example explicitly removes the shield, places body/head sprites, adjusts layers and considers multiple directions. Historical platform notes discuss animation loop/continuous behavior and correct y-order near level borders. These are legacy documents, not proof that current Classic or Era source code and all assets are publicly available. They nevertheless directly address the current quality concern: a held object, hand, shield and body must be authored as one coherent action. [Gani authoring](https://testwiki.graalonline.com/Creation/Dev/Gani), [legacy client release notes](https://testwiki.graalonline.com/Creation/Dev/Releases/Client/2).

## Proposed Charmville integration

**One party, two control presentations.** Keep the same owned creature UUID, HP, status, equipment and encounter identity in direct-world and command views. The command view is a tactical interface over that state, not another battle save. In shared space it must not pause everyone else. Start with one selected creature executing one acknowledged order; later allow a bounded squad budget. Six visible followers should never silently imply six simultaneous attacks or six independent loot rolls.

Use two accessible primary controls: interact/confirm and back/guard. A held modifier opens a compact command wheel: follow, hold, defend, focus target and retreat. Advanced controller bindings can directly select a companion or assist. Every order displays accepted, unavailable or interrupted feedback. “Hold” fixes an authorized reachable location; “defend” targets threats to the assigned ally; “focus” requires an explicit hostile target; “retreat” outranks offense. Neutral animals, visitors and public crops are excluded from autonomous aggression.

**Rules that players can understand.** Offer three editable behavior presets, initially simple: safe travel, support and encounter. Each contains a small ordered list of conditions and actions. Evaluate emergency retreat first, then permitted support, then the selected hostile target. Explicit player orders temporarily override routine rules and show when routine behavior resumes. Enforce cooldowns, range, line of sight, resource availability and ownership server-side. A rule editor should explain why no action occurred instead of hiding failures behind idle animation.

**Roles without species erasure.** Separate learned capability from assigned responsibility. A creature can be assigned protection, restoration, interruption or damage only when its actual move catalogue supports it. Species-specific animations and movement remain intact. A raid commander places rally marks and priorities; individual owners retain control of their companions. Formation offsets require navigable destinations and separation behavior. Existing close/relaxed trail spacing remains a travel feature until those constraints exist.

**Readable spectacle.** Define every action as anticipation, active interval, contact and recovery, with cancellation windows. Pair an authored ground shape with a cast indicator and a non-color cue. A beam has an origin socket, directional width, collision rule and expiry; a transform changes the character silhouette, pose, aura envelope, sound and move eligibility together. Keep environmental warning shapes visible beneath effects. Offer reduced flashes, bounded camera shake and adjustable allied-effect density. None of these display settings changes damage or timing.

**Authority and economic continuity.** A committed action event should carry actor/target UUIDs, move ID, timing, accepted position, outcome and applied damage. Clients deduplicate by event ID and animate that result; they cannot mint damage by playing effects. Transform activation consumes defined combat resources once, and expiry is authoritative. Consumable assists draw from inventory with receipts. Do not let automated companion routines issue unlimited gathering yield, grant remote access to another homestead, or multiply market supply merely because more sprites are visible.

**Delivery gates.** First prove one companion assist against one shared enemy: two accounts see the same target, windup, contact, HP change and recovery, including miss and interruption. Next verify formation movement around corners and through map transitions. Then add one transformation with source-backed pose coverage in every facing. Only after those gates add cooperative role exercises and a small raid encounter. Acceptance includes replay, disconnect, ownership change, rejected target, low-resource and reduced-effects cases. This sequence grows the shared game instead of adding visually persuasive but disconnected combat scenes.

## Source versions and limits

Sources were accessed 2026-09-09. FFXIV’s retrieved Job Guide labels itself updated for 7.5; its UI/manual pages are live documents without stable patch labels. FFXII references are the April 24, 2020 Square Enix-authored PS4 update and the Steam-distributed PC controls manual. Sparking! ZERO references are July 3 and September 26, 2024 publisher guides. FighterZ evidence is explicitly version 1.21. Graal Gani and client-2 documents are legacy platform-hosted technical references; their age limits claims about current tools. No unofficial forum opinion was used as authoritative gameplay specification, and no source establishes that the proposed Charmville systems are already complete.
