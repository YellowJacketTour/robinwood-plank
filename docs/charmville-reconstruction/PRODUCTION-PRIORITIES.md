## Dependency sequence

1. **Freeze coordinate semantics, then make one continuous playable region authoritative.** Define place, source-map position, actor footprint, collision revision, camera and entity anchors. Compile actual walkable boundaries and explicit portals from export. Migrate native observation/correction, beds, followers and encounters as one versioned cutover. Keep candidate testing separate until these consumers agree; extending a rectangle alone will recreate drifting props and invalid admissions.
2. **Finish entry-to-return in that same world.** Authored safe home, visible beds, two-button interaction, crop loop, starter, nearby wilderness encounter, return and reload. Validate sustained movement and transitions once per meaningful integrated change. UI narrative must name the actual admitted place and next valid action.
3. **Complete the Heart proof across game and social account.** Implement the existing Heart plan with one entitlement per profile, one visible crop, a real owned harvest and conserved gift. Then explicitly define the reaction sink. Record issuance, consumption and outstanding custody; do not promise growing market prices.
4. **Complete six-party hybrid expedition.** One server target and event history across world combat, staged scene, assist, capture and return. Add species/ball/status support by validated content definition. Six visible party members need collision-aware trails, readable attack origins and order feedback, not six independent overlays.
5. **Expand progression using those settled contracts.** Region difficulty and access, skill unlocks, gear/affix tiers, loot tables and personal party entitlements, resource professions, housing construction, livestock/breeding and crafting chains. Every new item requires acquisition, custody, use/sink, art and economic identity. Define solo, friends, competing parties and PvP/PvE ownership rules at introduction.
6. **Prove two independent accounts, then scale.** Home permission changes, shared public actors/creatures, simultaneous action outcomes, personal loot and competing captures. Only then load-test interest management, server tick/transport/persistence and spectators. Geography remains available as a persistent world while rendering and detailed subscriptions are bounded by relevance.
7. **Expand community authoring and social viewing.** Versioned asset packs with anchors, palette/theme tokens, animation states, provenance and compatibility; validated maps/recipes/entities; preview/review/publish/rollback. Video viewing uses server-enforced policy and separate media distribution. Voice/social tools share the in-game shell and explicit input focus.

## Acceptance and handoff discipline

Maintain one retained visible browser/account for normal play; identify synthetic fixture switches. Record source/content hash, loaded route, services and actual observed outcomes. Focused unit or transaction tests prove only their named contracts. The next useful proof is a continuous walk with fixed terrain/entities, then the complete home-to-expedition-to-return loop, not another exported asset count.

This audit made no gameplay changes, ran no tests and claims no new live verification. Root and other workers may change these surfaces concurrently; reconcile this checkpoint against their final changed-file list before presenting aggregate completion.
