## The beginning-to-end game must connect, not just coexist

1. Fresh profile enters a stable safe homestead, sees an intentional authored welcome, controls the hero immediately, learns target/context action, and can skip/replay narration without changing earned progress.
2. Tilling, seed, watering and harvest are distinct visible contacts; committed harvest advances a durable tutorial milestone. Merely owning an Oran or dismissing dialogue is not proof.
3. Choose a companion, see it follow, feed/use an owned item, learn battle/capture and retain the same unique identity in party, world and Charmdex.
4. Walk through a real home exit into a visibly different public map; meet another authenticated player, visit their authorized home, see their action, and return with unchanged ownership and valid safe placement.
5. Gather/craft/use/sell/react with conserved definitions across inventory, Satchel and Exchange. Menus must expose each action where the player expects it without leaving the game.
6. Professions, dungeons, public projects and creature growth unlock horizontal and tiered advancement, then regional/sea/sky/space travel. All content uses the same admission, action, ledger and art contracts.
7. Continuing endgame adds player creation, collections, specialization, factions/events and exploration. No finite implementation can certify an infinite content catalogue; the extensible contracts and representative authored paths must be executable.

Every stage needs solo, allied party, competing party, PvE and explicitly enabled PvP rules. Neutral homes never acquire hostile semantics accidentally. Capture locks, loot rights, helper credit and simultaneous interaction must be deterministic and communicated before the action. Disabled modes must be labeled unavailable rather than counted as built.
