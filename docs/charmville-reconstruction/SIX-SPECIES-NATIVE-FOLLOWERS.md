# Six species and trail spacing

The native renderer now supports Emerald IDs 277 Treecko, 280 Torchic, 283 Mudkip, 25 Pikachu, 133 Eevee and 286 Poochyena. Walking artwork comes from PMDCollab/SpriteCollab commit `db1928346e452e1a36b8ecff62f7e4d195504763`, folders 0252/0255/0258/0025/0133/0261. These are PMD walking sprites, not Emerald overworld sprites. The preserved source vault contains all six walking sheets, offsets, shadow markers, animation metadata and credits, plus repository license/readme and a 32-file hash manifest. Walk credits identify CHUNSOFT; retain the full credit files for other contributors and animation roles.

`acquire-follower-sprites.mjs` fetches the exact pinned files. `build-extra-follower-frames.mjs` reads source durations and one white ground marker per frame, generates `ExtraFollowerFrames.zh`, records frame dimensions, and preserves the source vault. `stage-action-sprites.mjs` performs the existing indexed/native palette conversion without modifying originals. The three added sheets use Pikachu 32x40 / 4 frames, Eevee 40x48 / 7 frames and Poochyena 32x48 / 5 frames. All 240 ground anchors across the six species pass the source-marker verifier.

The read-only parent projection accepts the six allowlisted IDs. A separate `{type:'charmville:follower-formation',formation:'close'|'relaxed'}` selects 18 or 26 world pixels of trail distance per slot. The native parser defaults to 18. Both presets follow accumulated player path segments and reset on warp; no arbitrary side offsets or wall shortcuts are introduced. This is trail spacing, not flanking, steering, attack/defend AI or raid combat.

The synthetic six-member native browser verifier passed all six draw markers, oversized-party rejection, clearing and both spacing values. Screenshots show the six distinct source sprites and the changed trail spacing; this fixture does not establish account ownership. Reversals can bunch or overlap followers because multiple slots can revisit the same location; avoidance remains unimplemented. A separate authenticated test-party flow must verify the parent projects owned UUIDs into these species selections.

## Immediate spawn visibility

Previously each slot stayed hidden until the player had walked 18–26 pixels per slot. Native startup and warp reset now seed at most 16 connected presentation anchors, checking a conservative 16px footprint against `Screen->isSolid` at each endpoint and midpoint. Repeated anchors are excluded; the normal observed player trail then takes over. This initial placement is cosmetic, not an authoritative creature position or a claim the player walked that path. Tight locations may safely fit fewer companions, and dynamic actor/FFC avoidance is not implemented.

`node scripts/charmville/verify-native-party-projection.mjs --spawn` passed with no directional input: all six distinct source species rendered at the stationary spawn, then a two-species selection rendered. The screenshots were visually inspected; the hero remains separate, with the party arranged along clear nearby ground. Source facing points back along the seeded trail. Candidate and published native compilation pass. This verifier exercises bounded read-only projection; authenticated roster selection is checked separately by the account/UI lane. Existing reversal bunching remains a limitation.

## Compressed species display sizes

Emerald's dex records Treecko/Poochyena at 5dm, Torchic/Mudkip/Pikachu at 4dm and Eevee at 3dm. Those values inform artistic classes; they do not prescribe upright silhouette height. `audit-follower-bounds.mjs` records all 488 opaque walk/attack frame bounds and original PNG hashes in `follower-opaque-bounds.json`. Walking maximum opaque heights are respectively 21,23,24,27,24,27px for Treecko,Torchic,Mudkip,Pikachu,Eevee,Poochyena. Transparent cell padding is excluded.

`FollowerScale.zh` applies fixed species factors to envelopes of 20px (5dm class),18px (4dm),16px (3dm), including the three supported attacks and the shared wild Poochyena. Ground markers scale with the output dimensions; factors never change per frame. Original PNGs, collision footprints, stats and authority are unchanged. These compressed chibi proportions favor readability: the roughly16px hero means Treecko can still appear taller, so this is explicitly not literal lore-to-human physical scale. Fractional pixel downsampling loses some detail; source originals remain available for future higher-resolution presentation.
